Four easy steps

  1. Boil pasta in salted water; reserve a mug of pasta water.
  2. Warm olive oil and gently cook the garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. Add spinach until wilted, then toss with lemon zest and juice.
  4. Mix in pasta and a splash of pasta water. Finish with Parmesan if you like.
